#+TITLE: Chasing Darkness Outside the City

From most suburbs the heavens are a washed-out grey; the glow thrown up by
streetlamps drowns all but the brightest handful of points overhead. Drive an
hour past the last shopping centre, though, and the Milky Way returns as a real
river of light spilled across the dark.

Let your eyes adjust for twenty minutes without glancing at a phone, and faint
constellations you never knew existed crowd in. A reclining chair and a thermos
turn the whole thing into an evening rather than a chore.
