#+TITLE: Bringing Home a Labrador Puppy

The crate should be ready before the Labrador arrives --- a den of its own
makes the first nights calmer. Expect early mornings: an eight-week-old puppy
needs to go outside within minutes of waking, and again after every meal and
play session.

Start the leash early, reward the wagging enthusiasm at the door with calm
rather than excitement, and keep training short. Chewing is not misbehavior; it
is how the young animal explores the world, so redirect it to a toy rather than
scolding. Months of patience buy you a steady, devoted creature that shadows
you from room to room for the next decade.
