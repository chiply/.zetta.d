#+TITLE: Taming a Credit Card That Got Away

The balance had a way of growing on its own once the interest compounded
faster than I paid it down. What finally worked was boring: write down every
outflow for a month, group them, and confront the total against what actually
lands in the account each pay cycle.

Two categories were quietly eating the difference. Cancelling one forgotten
subscription and packing lunch four days a week closed the gap, and for the
first time in a year the number on the statement started shrinking instead of
creeping upward.
