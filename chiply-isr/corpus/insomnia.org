#+TITLE: When Rest Refuses to Arrive

Lying in the dark, the day replays itself on a loop --- every unfinished task,
every awkward sentence. The harder you chase it, the further rest retreats.
What helps is lowering the stakes: dim the screens an hour beforehand, let the
room cool, and put the next morning's worries on paper so they stop circling.

If the rumination starts anyway, get up and read something dull under low light
rather than staring at the ceiling. The body finally drifts under once it stops
being ordered to.
