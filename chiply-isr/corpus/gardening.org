#+TITLE: Coaxing Tomatoes from a Balcony

You do not need an acre to grow your own. A sunny balcony rail and a few deep
containers are enough for cherry tomatoes, provided they catch at least six
hours of direct light. Use a pot wide enough to hold moisture through a hot
afternoon, stake the vines before they sprawl, and feed every couple of weeks
once the first fruit sets.

The harvest from four well-tended pots can outpace a neglected plot ten times
the size. City dwellers with nothing but a railing and a bag of compost are
often surprised how much they can put on the table.
