#+TITLE: From the Couch to a 5K

When I started, I could barely run for two minutes without stopping. The plan
was simple: alternate jogging and walking, and let the jogging intervals grow a
little each week. Don't try to go fast early --- a slow, conversational pace is
what builds the base.

By week eight most people can run the whole distance without a break, and only
then does it make sense to work on getting quicker. The trick is consistency,
not heroics: three easy runs a week beats one punishing sprint that leaves you
too sore to lace up again.
